(cites PERF-1, CAP-7, multiples_check)

Given the bank's strong performance in the first half of 2026, with net income of $17.658B compared to $14.53B in the prior period, the risk profile may be lower than the industry average, justifying a lower cost of equity and higher valuation. (cites PERF-8, cost_of_equity)
