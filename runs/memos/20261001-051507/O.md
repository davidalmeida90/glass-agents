## Red team

- [method, medium, rating too low, supported] The desk's valuation uses a revenue growth blend, but for a REIT like Realty Income, AFFO (Adjusted Funds From Operations) is the primary driver of dividend capacity and value. The guidance [GUID-2] shows strong AFFO growth, which is not captured by the revenue-only growth model, leading to an undervaluation of the dividend growth potential. (cites GUID-2)
- [data_conflict, high, rating too high, supported] The desk uses a revenue growth blend of 2.73% [growth_used]. However, the company's same-store rent growth guidance is significantly lower at 1.1% to 1.3% [GUID-3]. Relying on historical revenue growth that includes aggressive acquisitions [CAP-8] overstates the sustainable organic growth rate for the dividend discount model. (cites growth_used, GUID-3, CAP-8)
