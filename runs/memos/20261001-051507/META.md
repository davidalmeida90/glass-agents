## Red team

- [method, high, rating too high, supported] The desk's base fair value is significantly inflated by the terminal_excess_return assumption of 1.0, which assumes Meta will maintain its current wide-moat ROIC-WACC spread indefinitely. Given the intense competition in AI and regulatory risks (RISK-1, RISK-4), a more conservative terminal_excess_return of 0.5 is appropriate. (cites terminal_excess_return, RISK-1, RISK-4)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too low, supported] The desk's valuation relies on a 23.8% revenue growth rate, but the latest six-month performance (PERF-6) shows revenue of $117.1B vs $89.8B in the prior period, a growth rate of 30.4%. The desk's growth_used is too low, underestimating near-term momentum. (cites growth_used, PERF-6)
- [data_conflict, high, rating too low, supported] The desk's valuation assumes a constant operating margin of 20.9% to justify the current price, which is far below the company's historical performance and current segment profitability. Family of Apps alone achieved a 51.5% operating margin in 2025 (SEG-2/SEG-1). The desk's margin assumption is disconnected from reality. (cites operating_margin, SEG-1, SEG-2)
