While the desk uses a blend, the recent 10-Q data in [PERF-6] and [PERF-7] shows strong growth, but the massive increase in capital expenditures [GUID-3] suggests a potential drag on future free cash flow that the current model may not fully capture. (cites growth_used, implied_growth, PERF-6, PERF-7, GUID-3)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too low, supported] The desk's model requires a constant operating margin of 20.5% to justify the price at the modelled growth, which is significantly below the company's historical range (24.8% to 49.7%). This suggests the desk's model is overly conservative regarding the company's ability to maintain its operating efficiency, potentially leading to an undervalued rating.
