## Red team

- [method, medium, rating too high, supported] The desk's valuation assumes a terminal excess return of 1.0, which is the most sensitive input. Given the competitive retail environment and the risks associated with AI-driven cybersecurity and tariff pressures (RISK-1, RISK-5), assuming a wide moat that captures 100% of excess returns is overly optimistic and likely overstates the fair value. (cites terminal_excess_return, RISK-1, RISK-5)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too low, supported] The desk's model uses a starting revenue growth of 4.5%, which is lower than the company's own FY27 guidance of 4.0% to 5.0% (midpoint 4.5%) and significantly lower than the 6.6% growth observed in the most recent six-month period. The desk should reflect the stronger recent performance and guidance. (cites GUID-4, PERF-7)
