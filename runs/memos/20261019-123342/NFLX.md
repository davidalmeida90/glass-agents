## Red team

- [business, high, rating too high, supported] The desk's base fair value of 95.24 is too high because it ignores the significant financial risk associated with the WBD transaction. The company has committed to massive new debt facilities totaling over $67 billion (RISK-2, RISK-3, RISK-4), which drastically increases the company's risk profile and potential for dilution or financial distress, not captured in the current WACC. (cites RISK-2, RISK-3, RISK-4)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too high, supported] The desk's valuation uses a starting revenue growth of 13.82%, which is overly optimistic compared to the company's own 2026 guidance of 13%-14% (midpoint 13.5%) and the decelerating trend observed in Q3 guidance (12%). The growth input should be corrected to reflect the midpoint of the full-year guidance. (cites GUID-1, GUID-3)
- [business, medium, rating too high, supported] The desk's valuation assumes a terminal excess return based on a wide moat (1.0), but the company faces significant uncertainty regarding the sustainability of its competitive advantage due to the expiration of major guild agreements in 2026 and the legal uncertainty surrounding AI-generated content, which could disrupt its content production model and margins. (cites RISK-5, RISK-6)
