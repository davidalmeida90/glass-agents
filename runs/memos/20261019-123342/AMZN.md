## Red team

- [business, high, rating too low, supported] The desk's base fair value is overly sensitive to the operating margin assumption, which is the primary driver of the valuation. The desk assumes a constant 19.2% margin to justify the current price, which is significantly higher than the historical range (2.3% to 11.2%). This ignores the structural margin expansion potential from the high-growth, high-margin AWS segment. (cites PERF-8, SEG-5)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too low, supported] The desk's valuation relies on a trailing revenue growth of 12.1%, but fails to account for the accelerating growth in the AWS segment, which grew 33% in the first half of 2026. Given AWS's increasing share of total revenue, the consolidated growth rate is likely to be higher than the desk's 12.1% assumption. (cites PERF-10, SEG-4)
