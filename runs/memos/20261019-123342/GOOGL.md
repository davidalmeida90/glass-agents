- [business, high, rating too high, supported] The desk's valuation relies on a terminal excess return model that assumes a wide moat (terminal_excess_return = 1.0). However, significant legal and regulatory headwinds, including court-ordered remedies restricting service distribution and potential structural remedies in advertising technology (RISK-5, RISK-6), threaten the sustainability of Alphabet's competitive advantage, suggesting a lower terminal_excess_return is appropriate. (cites RISK-5, RISK-6, terminal_excess_return)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too low, supported] The desk's valuation assumes a starting revenue growth of 20.1%. However, the trailing twelve months revenue growth calculated from the latest 10-Q (PERF-6) shows a growth rate of 23.05% (229,692 / 186,662 - 1). This higher growth momentum is not reflected in the desk's growth_used, leading to an undervaluation of the company's near-term prospects. (cites PERF-6, growth_used)
