## Red team

- [business, high, rating too low, supported] The desk's valuation significantly underestimates growth potential. Guidance for FY 2027 revenue of at least $90 billion (GUID-3) implies a growth rate substantially higher than the 21.1% used in the model, especially given the 33% growth observed in Q1 FY 2027 (PERF-8). The current rating is too low. (cites GUID-3, PERF-8, growth_used)
- [business, medium, rating too low, supported] The desk's operating margin assumption is overly conservative. The company achieved a 59% margin in its primary Cloud and Software segment (PERF-3), and the desk's requirement of 36.7% to justify the price ignores the high-margin nature of the cloud transition. The rating is too low given the strong segment performance. (cites PERF-3, operating_margin)
