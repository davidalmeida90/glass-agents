## Red team

- [business, medium, rating too high, supported] The desk's valuation assumes a terminal excess return of 1.0, which is the most sensitive input. However, the company faces significant competitive risks from generative and agentic AI, which could erode its long-term competitive advantage and moat, making a 1.0 terminal excess return overly optimistic. (cites RISK-1, RISK-2, terminal_excess_return)
- [data_conflict, high, rating too low, supported] The desk's valuation implies a constant operating margin of 13.4% to justify the current price, which is significantly below the company's historical 10-year range of 25.5% to 36.8% and the 2026 guidance of 35.0%. This suggests the market is pricing in an overly pessimistic margin outlook that contradicts the company's demonstrated operational efficiency. (cites GUID-8, operating_margin_reported)
