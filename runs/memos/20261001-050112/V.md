## Red team

- [data_conflict, high, rating too low, supported] The desk's base fair value is overly pessimistic because it assumes a constant operating margin of 44.2% to justify the current price, which is significantly below Visa's historical performance (60.0% to 65.7%). The evidence shows strong revenue growth (11% in fiscal 2025) and high profitability, suggesting the margin assumption is disconnected from reality. (cites PERF-2)
- [method, medium, rating too low, supported] The terminal_excess_return assumption of 1.0 (wide moat) is appropriate given Visa's dominant market position and consistent profitability, but the desk's DCF model fails to capture the full value of this moat by forcing an unrealistic margin compression. The valuation should reflect the company's ability to maintain its high operating margins. (cites PERF-5, PERF-8)
