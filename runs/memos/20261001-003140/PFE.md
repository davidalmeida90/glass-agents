## Red team

- [data_conflict, high, rating too high, supported] The desk's growth_used of -0.21% comes from a 10-K fiscal year that is already stale: the 10-Q shows Q2 2026 revenue +3% to $15.0bn and first-half 2026 +4% to $29.5bn. Using a declining trailing figure understates the current trajectory and biases the DCF downward, making the Underweight look more justified than the evidence supports. (cites PERF-3, PERF-4, growth_used)
- [business, high, rating too low, supported] The rating ignores that the core Biopharma segment generated $61.2bn of $62.6bn total revenue and $29.3bn of earnings in 2025, a ~48% segment margin. The consolidated operating_margin of 24.7% masks a highly profitable franchise, so the base fair value of $22.58 may be too pessimistic relative to the segment economics. (cites PERF-8, PERF-9, operating_margin)
- [method, medium, rating too high, supported] The terminal value assumes 50.6% of today's ROIC-WACC spread survives (terminal_share 0.506), but current ROIC of 7.92% is already below WACC of 8.09%, so the excess return is negative. Applying a positive moat share to a negative spread is internally inconsistent and may overstate terminal value. (cites roic, wacc, terminal_share, terminal_excess_return)
- [method, medium, rating too low, supported] The method capitalizes R&D and adds a 3-point margin adjustment, lifting operating_margin to 24.69% from a reported 21.69%. If the R&D capitalization life or amortization schedule is aggressive, the adjusted margin overstates true profitability, and the resulting fair value could be understated by the reinvestment charge. (cites operating_margin, operating_margin_reported, rd_margin_adjustment)
