## Red team

- [data_conflict, high, rating too high, supported] The desk clips starting growth at 30% (growth_bounds), but the latest twelve months grew 83.4% (revenue_growth) and Q2 FY27 revenue rose 106% (PERF-10). Even the 10-K's 65% (PERF-2) exceeds the clip. Capping at 30% while implied_growth is only 19.1% understates the near-term trajectory the evidence shows. (cites revenue_growth, PERF-10, PERF-2, growth_bounds, implied_growth)
- [business, high, rating too low, supported] The rating ignores that NVDA is effectively foreclosed from China's data center market (RISK-5) and faces a 15% USG revenue share expectation (RISK-3) plus 25% H200 tariffs (RISK-4). These are structural revenue and margin headwinds the base case does not haircut, so fair value may be overstated, not understated. (cites RISK-5, RISK-3, RISK-4, RISK-1)
- [method, medium, rating too low, supported] The moat test uses terminal_excess_return = 0.5 (narrow) from 5-year ROIC history, but roic is 62.1% against wacc 10.96% — a spread far above moat_margin. If the wide-moat share (1.0) applied, terminal value and base fair value would rise materially, pushing price/base below the Hold band. (cites terminal_excess_return, roic, wacc, moat_margin, terminal_share)
- [method, medium, rating too high, supported] cash_conversion is 0.749, below the 0.8 caution threshold, and the desk already flags a one-notch downgrade for it. Yet the rating stays Hold; if cash conversion is a genuine quality signal, the effective rating should be lower than the stated band implies. (cites cash_conversion, ACC-2, ACC-1)
