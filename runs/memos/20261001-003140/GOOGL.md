## Red team

- [business, high, rating too low, supported] Q2 2026 revenue grew 24% y/y (PERF-2) and Cloud revenue nearly doubled to $44.8bn in H1 2026 (PERF-10), yet growth_used is 0.2005 and the model decays it to 10% by year 5. The 10-K evidence shows accelerating, not fading, momentum; the desk's Hold understates this. (cites PERF-2, PERF-10, growth_used)
- [method, medium, rating too low, supported] The year5_growth_cap of 0.1 forces a 20% grower to decay to 10% within five years, and terminal growth is capped at the 4.75% risk-free rate. For a company with a 22% ROIC and wide moat, this fade is aggressive and mechanically depresses base fair value. (cites year5_growth_cap, terminal_growth_cap, roic)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too high, supported] Operating margin is 38.5% R&D-adjusted but only 33.1% reported, and Q2 2026 margin fell to 32% from 34% (PERF-3). The capitalized-R&D uplift flatters margins; on the reported basis the margin trend is deteriorating, so the base case may be too generous. (cites PERF-3, operating_margin, operating_margin_reported)
- [business, medium, rating too high, supported] Capex jumped to $91.4bn in 2025 from $52.5bn (CAP-7) and AI infrastructure is constrained by power, water, land and limited chip suppliers (RISK-1, RISK-2). Rising capital intensity and supply bottlenecks threaten the sales_to_capital of 0.787 and the 22% ROIC underpinning the base value. (cites CAP-7, RISK-1, RISK-2, sales_to_capital)
