## Red team

- [method, high, rating too high, supported] The DCF charges reinvestment at sales-to-capital of 1.241, but 2025 capex was $128.3bn (CAP-6) against ~$113bn of revenue growth — roughly 1.1x, and far above the 1.241 implied by a business whose growth is increasingly AWS (PERF-4, +20%). Understated reinvestment inflates FCFF and the base value, so the Sell is too harsh. (cites CAP-6, PERF-4, sales_to_capital)
- [data_conflict, high, rating too low, supported] growth_used of 15.77% is the TTM figure, but the 10-K shows 12% FY2025 (PERF-2) and the 10-Q shows 18% for the six months to June 2026 (PERF-9). The most recent verified momentum is accelerating, not decelerating; the model's 5-year decay to 10% understates the starting point and the base value. (cites PERF-9, PERF-2, growth_used)
- [business, medium, rating too low, supported] AWS grew 20% to $128.7bn (PERF-4) and International 13% (PERF-5), yet the model applies one blended 15.77% growth and a single 12.08% margin. Mix shift toward high-margin AWS should lift consolidated margin above the 12.08% used, raising fair value and arguing against Sell. (cites PERF-4, PERF-5, operating_margin)
- [method, medium, rating too high, supported] Terminal ROIC of 9.44% sits below WACC of 7.85% only barely, and terminal_share of 0.877 means nearly all value is terminal. With moat share 0.877 the model assumes AWS's excess return persists forever; if the fade is faster, base fair value falls well below 145.33, making Sell too generous. (cites terminal_roic, terminal_share, wacc)
