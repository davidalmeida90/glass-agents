## Red team

- [method, medium, rating too high, supported] The DCF's own implied_growth of 19.24% is far above the 8.03% growth_used and the 6% fiscal-2025 figure (PERF-2). A model that needs ~19% growth to justify today's price is not evidence the price is only 1.35x fair value; it shows the base case may be too generous, not too harsh. (cites implied_growth, growth_used, PERF-2)
- [business, high, rating too low, supported] The 10-K shows accelerating momentum the desk's 8.03% blend understates: nine-month 2026 sales rose 16% (PERF-6) and operating cash flow jumped to $116,996m from $81,754m (PERF-8), with net income up 20% (PERF-7). Europe grew 10% (PERF-10). Growth_used should reflect this, not the older 6% fiscal year. (cites PERF-6, PERF-7, PERF-8, PERF-10)
- [method, medium, rating too low, supported] The terminal value assumes only 68.9% of today's 58.6% ROIC-WACC spread survives (terminal_share), yet the 5-year ROIC history is wide-moat and the moat is intact. If the moat share were 1.0, terminal value and base fair value would rise materially, pushing price/fair value below the Underweight threshold. (cites terminal_share, roic, terminal_roic, terminal_excess_return)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too high, supported] The desk's operating_margin of 36.87% is R&D-adjusted; the reported GAAP margin is 33.17% (operating_margin_reported). Gross margin is only 46.9% (PERF-3), so a 36.9% operating margin implies implausibly thin opex. The higher margin flatters fair value and understates the rating. (cites operating_margin, operating_margin_reported, PERF-3)
