## Red team

- [data_conflict, high, rating too high, supported] The desk's growth_used of 14.24% is the trailing-twelve-month figure, but the 10-K evidence shows fiscal 2025 net sales grew only 6% (PERF-2) to $416,161m (PERF-1). The desk's own growth_bounds clip to the last twelve months, so a 6% base rate would cut the starting growth by more than half and lower fair value materially. (cites PERF-2, PERF-1, growth_used, growth_bounds)
- [business, high, rating too low, supported] Services, the highest-margin, most recurring revenue line, grew 14% to $109,158m (PERF-9) and now exceeds a quarter of sales. The desk's single blended growth rate and 36.87% operating margin ignore this mix shift, which should lift both growth and margin versus the 10-K's 6% total. (cites PERF-9, PERF-4, operating_margin)
- [method, medium, rating too high, supported] The terminal value keeps 70.8% of today's excess return (terminal_share 0.708) with terminal ROIC of 58.63% forever, far above the 10.19% WACC. That is an aggressive moat assumption for a hardware company facing tariffs (RISK-1) and platform regulation (RISK-5, RISK-6); a lower moat share would cut the base fair value sharply. (cites terminal_share, terminal_roic, RISK-1, RISK-5, RISK-6)
- [method, medium, rating too low, supported] The method expenses SBC (2.94% of revenue) and capitalizes R&D, but the 10-K shows $111,482m operating cash flow against $112,010m net income (PERF-6, PERF-5) and cash_conversion of 1.031, so owner earnings are understated; the DCF penalizes a business that converts essentially all earnings to cash. (cites PERF-6, PERF-5, cash_conversion, sbc_to_revenue)
