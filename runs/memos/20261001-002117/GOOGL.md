## Red team

- [method, medium, rating too high, supported] The moat test awards terminal_share 0.761 (wide moat) on a 5-year ROIC history, but the model's own terminal ROIC of 22.15% is held against a WACC that fades to 8.75%. A 13-point perpetual spread on a $400bn-revenue base is the single largest driver of base fair value; the evidence gives no basis for assuming it survives regulation (RISK-1, RISK-2, RISK-5) or the power/land/chip constraints (RISK-3, RISK-4). (cites terminal_share, terminal_roic, RISK-3, RISK-5)
- [business, medium, rating too low, supported] The desk's growth_used of 20.05% is clipped by growth_bounds and then decayed to the 10% year-5 cap, but the 10-K shows the mix shifting to Cloud ($58.7bn, PERF-3) and advertising at $294.7bn (PERF-4) with capex up from $52.5bn to $91.4bn (CAP-7) — capacity being built, not a business already at its growth ceiling. The fade may be too fast. (cites PERF-3, PERF-4, CAP-7, growth_used)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too low, supported] Net debt is entered as -$124.3bn, but the 10-K shows cash and marketable securities of $126.8bn (CAP-1) against debt including leases of $118.2bn — a net figure near zero, not a $124bn net cash cushion. If the bridge overstates net cash, base fair value is understated and the Hold is too low. (cites CAP-1, net_debt, fair_value)
- [method, low, rating too high, supported] SBC is expensed at 6.31% of revenue (sbc_to_revenue) yet buybacks of $45.4bn (CAP-5) with $69.5bn remaining (CAP-8) offset dilution; the model charges the cost but gives no credit for the capital returned, and cash_conversion of 1.324 (PERF-9 vs PERF-7) suggests owner earnings are understated, not overstated. (cites sbc_to_revenue, CAP-5, CAP-8, cash_conversion)
