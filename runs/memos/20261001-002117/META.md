## Red team

- [data_conflict, high, rating too high, supported] The desk's starting growth of 27.65% (growth_used) is the trailing-twelve-month figure, but the 10-K shows fiscal 2025 revenue growth of 22% (PERF-2). The 10-K is the audited, verified basis; using 27.65% inflates the first five years of the DCF and the base fair value. (cites PERF-2, growth_used)
- [business, high, rating too high, supported] Reality Labs lost $19.19bn in 2025 (PERF-9) on $2.21bn of revenue (PERF-7), while Family of Apps earned $102.47bn (PERF-8). The 52.28% operating margin (operating_margin) is a consolidated figure that assumes the loss-making segment never worsens; the desk's margin is not sustainable if AI/metaverse spending continues. (cites PERF-9, PERF-7, PERF-8, operating_margin)
- [business, medium, rating too low, supported] Cash generation is far stronger than the DCF implies: operating cash flow was $115.8bn (PERF-5) against $60.46bn net income (PERF-4), a cash_conversion of 1.403. Capex of $72.22bn (CAP-7) is discretionary and partly growth investment; the desk's reinvestment charge may overstate the true cash cost, understating fair value. (cites PERF-5, PERF-4, CAP-7, cash_conversion)
- [method, medium, rating too low, supported] The terminal value assumes terminal_share of 0.786 of value and terminal ROIC of 23.39% with a wide-moat share of 1.0, yet the fade caps growth at the risk-free rate (terminal_growth 4.75%) and forces WACC up toward mature_wacc. These fixed assumptions mechanically depress the base case for a company with a proven 23.39% ROIC. (cites terminal_share, terminal_roic, terminal_growth, mature_wacc)
