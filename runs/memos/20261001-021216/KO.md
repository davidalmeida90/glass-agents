## Red team

- [method, medium, rating too high, supported] The moat share is set to 1.0 (terminal_excess_return), so the terminal value keeps 100% of today's ROIC-WACC spread forever, while terminal_share is already 0.736. A 0.5 moat share (narrow, not wide) would cut base fair value materially and push price/base further above the Sell line. (cites terminal_excess_return, terminal_share, roic, wacc)
- [business, medium, rating too low, supported] The desk's growth_used of 4.4% ignores the latest 10-Q: concentrate sales volume +6% and unit case volume +4% (PERF-2), with gross margin up to 62.9% (PERF-3) and H1 net income $8,404m (PERF-5). Momentum is accelerating, not fading, so the 5-year plateau at 4.4% understates the near-term cash flows. (cites PERF-2, PERF-3, PERF-5, growth_used)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too low, supported] The equity bridge uses net_debt of $34,956m, but CAP-5 shows debt including current portion of $43,941m against cash of $13,872m (CAP-2) — roughly $30bn net. The larger debt figure, plus leases, would lower equity value and support the Sell; the smaller one flatters it. (cites net_debt, CAP-5, CAP-2, CAP-4)
- [method, low, rating too high, unsupported] The DCF is triangulated against industry EV/EBIT of 20.1x giving $59.09/share, yet the company trades at market_ev_to_ebit 28.4x versus the model's own ev_to_ebit 21.9x. The multiples check (dcf_to_multiples 1.099) is inside the 0.67-1.5 band, so it adds no caution despite the wide gap. (cites market_ev_to_ebit, ev_to_ebit, dcf_to_multiples, multiples_check)
