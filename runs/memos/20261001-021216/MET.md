## Red team

- [method, medium, rating too high, supported] The excess-return model starts from a normalized 5-year average ROE, but the desk's roe of 0.1162 sits barely above implied_roe 0.1103 and below the industry's 0.1287. With financial_moat_cap 0.5, the terminal spread is halved, so the base value leans on a spread that is thin and cyclical — the rating's Underweight rests on a fragile excess return. (cites roe, implied_roe, multiples_check)
- [method, medium, rating too low, supported] The multiples check values MET at 55.89 per share versus price 95.20, i.e. DCF/multiples 1.377 — the second method says the shares are worth far less than the market, yet the desk's own band treats 1.24x base as merely Underweight. If the industry P/B of 1.43 and industry ROE 0.1287 are the right anchors, the fair value is understated relative to peers. (cites multiples_check, price, book_value_per_share)
- [business, medium, rating too low, supported] Operating cash flow was $17,092m against net income of $3,403m (PERF-4, PERF-3) — roughly 5x earnings — while buybacks of $2.9bn and dividends of $1.5bn were funded comfortably (CAP-5, CAP-6). The rating ignores this cash generation, which supports a higher value than a book-value-anchored model implies. (cites PERF-4, PERF-3, CAP-5, CAP-6)
- [method, low, rating too high, supported] The cost of equity of 0.0742 uses a bottom-up beta of 0.644, well below the regression beta of 0.855 (raw 0.784). With beta_disagreement 0.3 the guard is not triggered, but the low beta mechanically lifts the justified P/B and the fair value; a regression-based beta would lower the base value and push the rating further toward Sell. (cites cost_of_equity, fair_value)
