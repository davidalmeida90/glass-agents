- [data_conflict, high, rating too high, supported] The desk's starting growth of 5.68% (growth_used) is below the 6.5% net sales growth for the six months ended July 31, 2026 (PERF-9), the most recent period, and below the 4.7% FY26 figure only after blending. The TTM period is accelerating, so the DCF understates near-term cash flows and the Underweight is too harsh. (cites PERF-9, PERF-2, growth_used)
- [business, high, rating too low, supported] The desk's operating margin of 4.43% (operating_margin) is far below the segment economics: Walmart U.S. alone earned $25,158m operating income on $482,975m net sales (5.2%), and gross profit rate rose 8bp (PERF-3). A 4.43% consolidated margin understates the true earning power and depresses fair value. (cites PERF-6, PERF-5, PERF-3, operating_margin)
- [method, medium, rating too high, supported] The DCF/multiples check is 0.578, far outside the 0.67-1.5 agreement band, yet the desk only bumps uncertainty one level. The industry EV/EBIT of 32.4x (multiples_check) implies $124.21/share versus a $71.80 base, so the DCF may be structurally too low and the Underweight mis-scaled. (cites multiples_check, ev_to_ebit, market_ev_to_ebit, fair_value)
- [method, medium, rating too low, supported] Terminal value assumes only 75.1% of today's excess return survives (terminal_share) despite ROIC of 16.13% versus WACC of 7.86% — a 8.3-point spread sustained across the 5-year history. The moat is being faded too aggressively, cutting terminal value and the base fair value. (cites terminal_share, roic, wacc, terminal_roic)
