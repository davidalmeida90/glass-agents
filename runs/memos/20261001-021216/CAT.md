If the 24% Q2 print (PERF-5) is the true run-rate, the model's 5-year plateau-then-fade is too punitive and base fair value is understated. (cites implied_growth, PERF-5, growth_used)

For a franchise earning 26.6% ROIC (roic) with 1.31x cash conversion, the fade is too aggressive and depresses base fair value. (cites terminal_roic, terminal_share, roic, cash_conversion, terminal_growth)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too high, supported] The desk's operating_margin of 18.11% (R&D-adjusted) sits below the 19.4% H1 2026 reported margin (PERF-7) and above FY25's 16.5% (PERF-2).
