## Red team

- [data_conflict, high, rating too low, supported] The desk's starting growth of 30% (growth_used) is far below the company's actual trajectory: fiscal 2026 revenue grew 65% (PERF-2) and Q2 fiscal 2027 revenue grew 106% year over year (PERF-7). Even the 3-year blend cannot justify 30% when the latest quarter is accelerating above 100%. (cites PERF-2, PERF-7, growth_used)
- [method, medium, rating too low, unsupported] The year5_growth_cap of 10% forces a company growing 65-106% to decay linearly to 10% by year 5, then fade to 4.75%. This mechanically caps the DCF far below the multiples check (ev_to_ebit 30.9 vs market 25.7; multiples value_per_share 362.49 vs price 227.21), which is why dcf_to_multiples is only 0.752. (cites PERF-2, PERF-7, year5_growth_cap, multiples_check, dcf_to_multiples)
- [business, high, rating too high, supported] The rating ignores severe regulatory/geopolitical risk: a $4.5bn H20 charge (RISK-1), effective foreclosure from China's data center market (RISK-5), a 25% H200 import tariff (RISK-4), and USG demands for 15%+ of licensed revenue (RISK-3). These threaten the very growth the DCF capitalizes. (cites RISK-1, RISK-3, RISK-4, RISK-5)
- [data_conflict, medium, rating too high, supported] Cash conversion is 0.749, below the desk's own 0.8 caution threshold, and the desk already applied a one-notch penalty. But net income of $120,067m (ACC-1) exceeds operating cash flow of $102,718m (ACC-2), and inventories plus purchase obligations of $95.2bn (ACC-7) signal earnings quality risk the Hold rating understates. (cites ACC-1, ACC-2, ACC-7, cash_conversion)
